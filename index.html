About Me

My journey from mechanical engineering to full-stack development has been filled with passion, learning, and lots of love for creating beautiful, functional solutions.

From Gears to Code ✨

My journey began in the world of mechanical engineering, where I was trained to design systems with precision and problem-solving at the core. As part of our university curriculum, we were required to complete an internship during our engineering studies. That’s when I enrolled in a Java Full Stack & Web Development course, offered as a college-arranged internship program.

Initially, it was just about fulfilling academic requirements—but something unexpected happened. I found myself genuinely fascinated by coding. The logic, creativity, and constant learning excited me in ways I hadn’t felt before. By the time I graduated, it was clear: my passion had shifted toward technology.

To build on that spark, I took a bold step and joined JSpiders, where I received in-depth training in Java, Spring Boot, and modern web development. The transition from mechanical systems to software architecture felt natural—both fields thrive on structured thinking, precision, and building things that function seamlessly.

Now, I combine my engineering mindset with a deep appreciation for code, crafting scalable applications that not only work beautifully but also bring real value to users.
